## 1. What requires an actual state invariant

The scalar inequality `L ≥ D + H` does not by itself specify a physically executable allocation. It must be attached to disjoint original pieces, actual owner bijections, actual allocated prefixes, their exact private complements, a partition of original root IDs among packages, and a graph of realizable equalizer assignments. In particular, a frozen root cannot become a donor again, an old private complement cannot be borrowed by another root, and a formal suffix returned by the masked primitive is not automatically the physical material removed.

The intended state has one still-live original per live package, with immutable frozen ancestors. Every live assignment is a perfect matching in that root's visited graph. An edge `p→j` means the actual prefix is the stored equalizer `T(p,j)`, of cost exactly the recipient's original baseline. Every original label contributes at most one allocated prefix; its complement is a literal set difference inside that same original label. Root and label domains are disjoint, so summing package costs does not identify or duplicate material.

The graph is a union of complete matchings, not an arbitrary directed graph. Each edge of a matching lies on a directed cycle of that matching. Hence an edge cannot leave its strongly connected component in the union. Strong connectivity gives an old simple return path entirely inside that component. This is the fact needed for the new-edge cycle construction; scalar payments cannot replace it.

For each live label, allowed recipients, forbidden recipients, the stored frontier, and the frontier's eligible-order index are all necessary. An allowed recipient's equalizer contains the frontier. Every eligible mark already passed in the local order belongs to the current source component. All original atom sets to the right of the frontier are small. The order index remains necessary when different agents' marks coincide physically.

## 2. A genuine local-to-global composition distinction

One group advance changes its survivor's component partition. Other as-yet-unprocessed groups retain the old partition. Therefore a single completed group is not itself a new globally committed pool state.

For example, take two disjoint groups whose old graphs have singleton components, and use a legal two-component cycle on each. After processing the first group, its survivor has component `{0,1}`, whereas roots waiting in the second group still have separate components `{0}` and `{1}`. Any assertion that all live graphs share the merged partition at this intermediate instant is false. This is a structural scheduling witness, not a counterexample to the intended whole-pool algorithm or a new allocation lower bound.

The required rule is precise: reserve a disjoint partition into groups from the old committed pool; use the same old common component cycle for every group; keep all resulting survivors inactive; close the leftover old packages; and only then replace the live pool, common partition, representatives, and pending proposals in one commit. No staged survivor may enter a later old group, be revealed, or be used as another donor before that commit. Each group can use its own root-local paths and its own recorded next agents.

The phrase “arbitrary interleavings” can concern the legal sequence of committed revelations and committed whole-pool mergers, with arbitrary allowed cycle/group choices. It cannot authorize interleaving individual groups with newly revealed or reused survivors. Nor does a termination claim concern an artificial schedule that repeats free resets forever instead of taking a required progress step.

## 3. Reset and closure rederived from actual costs

For a live root, resetting to a perfect matching of its graph gives each recipient its own stored baseline. A label's actual observer deficit is bounded by its allowed-recipient reserve. The operation may reattach or detach original material, but only within that root's own original label; it never draws from a frozen ancestor or the outer residue. The old frozen credits and deficits do not change.

Closing a root preserves its current actual allocation. Each of its new frozen deficits is bounded by the old live reserve. The root adds no own credit, because its recipients held equalizers. Removing its live reserve after adding those actual deficits therefore preserves the inequality. These statements also hold for a root closed by scarcity, filtering, or an unused group remainder.

Positive surplus follows from an exact decomposition of each rival's cost into positive and negative baseline deviations. Each rival receives one label per root, so the negative parts are exactly the indicator-weighted label deficits. Their sum is paid by the frozen own reductions. The own-cost identity is essential: subtracting it leaves all rival positive deviations available as real surplus, including the deviations in frozen ancestors whose eligible sets differ from the live root's set.

## 4. Ordinary group advance: material and mutually exclusive payments

The masked primitive has one task per actual observer in the disjoint source components. Each task values only its own label block. Its nominal suffix meets that block in a whole suffix, a proper suffix, or the empty set. The actual removed material is only this intersection. Positive material in irrelevant nominal blocks is neither assigned nor removed by the physical chore operation.

Every nominal donor root is reserved for the entire group, including a zero-own assignment. A positive clipped own utility equals the exact affine reduction `C_i(E)−g(i,p)`. A zero clipped proposal is not executed: a negative affine value cannot be treated as zero physical own cost. In the fallback, these zero nominees still freeze unchanged. Every nominal donor has a distinct original root, so each root has at most one modified label beyond an equalizer matching.

For a positive donor assigned to j on label p, the new edge `p→j` and an old simple `j→p` path within the source component form one cycle. Self edges complete the owner bijection. Labels on the old return path use their original recipient equalizers; only p uses the actual complement `P_p\E_j`. Thus only the designated recipient acquires a new own reduction. All other recipients preserve their original baselines.

The exact offset matters even when E intersects material that was already private. The complete suffix E is physically outside the final prefix, but only `C_i(E)−g(i,p)` is the new own reduction. A formerly private part outside E may be reattached; it is not another extracted item. The identity `P_p=(P_p\E) ⊔ E` is the material statement required.

For a fixed observer i in the advancing source component C, split rivals into two exclusive cases. A rival j inside C can incur the one modified-p donor deficit, which same-label primitive envy-freeness bounds by i's new credit. Its survivor reserve does not grow: an already allowed value remains fixed, and a previously unallowed internal recipient becomes forbidden. A rival outside C receives no modified-p donor, but its new survivor reserve is bounded by comparison against the unused whole parent. One credit is therefore never being spent on the sum of two separately bounded obligations.

When i's own clipped value is zero, both required bounds are zero. Stripping that nominal assignment loses no necessary payment. For i outside C, its eligible equalizer lies at or left of the first outside eligible mark. If its omitted equalizer lay on the wrong side, its large original gap would be a sum of at most n small atoms, contradicting `H²>n`. Hence none of the offered suffixes produces a new deficit for this observer.

In the survivor, old paths connect the incoming agent to the departing representative inside each old component. These paths lie in disjoint components, so they combine with the new intercomponent edges into a simple cycle and a complete owner matching. The new graph merges exactly those components. The new next-agent index is beyond the old frontier index; all skipped eligible agents were internal. New forbidden recipients lie in the old source component and cannot include the newly added outside recipient. These facts establish the graph, geometric, and forbidden-set portions of the invariant, not just its cost inequality.

## 5. Atomic and exhausted closures

An atomic scan uses the first large original atom reached from the old frontier. Every atom to its right is small. Retaining all original equalizer boundaries ensures that no omitted mark can hide inside that atom. The participating set A consists of eligible marks at or to its right endpoint, and lies inside the current source component. Every such recipient has an old return path.

The closed primitive uses affine utilities with nonnegative whole values, rather than clipped utilities. Every assigned output, including affine-zero and physically empty outputs, is executed and retained. This distinction is necessary to preserve an actually whole parent. Nonnegative own affine reductions pay all new positive donor deficits. Outside A, an eligible equalizer lies at or left of the atom, while an omitted equalizer on the wrong side would contradict the small-atom/large-gap separation. Other labels consume old reserves. No future live reserve is created because every selected package and every other package closes.

The extracted X is the union of the complete actual assigned suffixes, including any of their formerly private portions. A whole assigned parent contains one entire first-large atom; its common observer mask supplies the lower bound on X. The complement of every assigned suffix remains allocated. In the unassigned root, the old allowed prefix contains the old frontier and therefore the unprocessed large atom. Consequently no unextracted large material leaks into the residual domain. Other released pieces are small.

For exhausted termination, A is the entire eligible set, not merely a subset of formerly visited owners. The new paid closed cycle may assign p to a previously forbidden recipient. This is permissible only because no live state survives. Each participant has a distinct assigned root, even for zero or empty outputs. For every observer outside A, that root's large original gap minus at most n small removed atoms is a positive deviation. The surplus lemma converts these separate root witnesses into actual margins against every participant, including all ancestral costs. Those margins cover the whole remaining residue, which may then be divided among the complementary smaller population.

The physical residue bound counts original atom sets in the final union, once each, rather than once per reset. Frozen complements are never restored. The outer reference residue remains untouched until a closed certificate; all Core tails belong either to later allocated snapshots/clearance outputs or to that exact outer residue. This is why local restoration never authorizes another Core call on released package material.

## 6. Pending proposals, floors, and recursive coverage

A revelation changes no surviving root's graph or frontier. Closing and filtering retain only a subset of roots and restrict pending records to it. If another component cycle is committed, the representative, graph, frontier, and eligible set of an unaffected source component remain unchanged. Its actual current matching can reset to identity, but that is irrelevant to its stored next-agent/suffix proposal. If its target component merges, the stored next agents all belong to the same new target union, still outside the unchanged source. Anchoring the target by an actual label avoids a stale component identifier.

Revealing an unrevealed component once, and deleting only the proposals of components that merge, gives at most n+G revelations. A whole-pool merger decreases the number of components, so G≤n−1. These are prefix statements about legal committed operations. Once all components have pending external targets, their functional graph has a cycle of length at least two. At the full component the protected representative retains its omitted cutter, forcing a proper closed certificate rather than a vacuous full-population exhausted set.

At a continuing revelation, at most B₀ scarce roots close and one of at most n target-component classes survives. At a committed merger, disjoint groups each have d+1≤n+1 roots, and every remainder closes. These yield the literal floors `floor((M−B₀)/n)` and `floor(M/(d+1))`. They do not assume divisibility or count staged survivors twice. The reserve `(B₀+n²+1)(n+1)^t` survives either operation with one unit removed. The final base exceeds all scarcity and closed-batch requirements.

After a closed certificate, the exact remaining domain is either dominated for a smaller population or split into the two separated domains used by the in-paper completion. Additions are on disjoint complements. Inactive agents are protected by whole-domain margins, so later endpoint recording only for active observers does not discard a needed ancestor comparison. Child populations are nonempty and smaller. Thus the package-stage preservation is the missing compositional connection to the finite recursion, rather than a new arithmetic estimate alone.
