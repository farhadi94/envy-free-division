**Protocol and cost semantics.** `OracleProtocol.run` (line 51) executes the same tape/history rule against actual Eval/Cut answers. Its fuel test applies only to requests, so a return or query-free divergence after the last allowed answer is retained. A terminal illegal request is exposed with one more unit of fuel but is never counted as an answer. `queryCost` (line 290) is the supremum of all finite-cap answered counts. Thus it charges unsuccessful runs, assigns infinite cost to infinitely many answers, and permits finite-cost query-free divergence.

The derived replay theorem preserves answered replies and the possible terminal illegal request; it is not a field of `Protocol`. `NonnegativeOracle.cut_reply_preserved` uses the left-to-returned-root integral for a successful Cut. Strict positivity makes that root unique and proves legality on the perturbed profile without also preserving the bracket total. The attempted-cost definition in `NonnegativeMain` is the supremum of the actual answered-query list plus the possible exposed illegal request. An unconsulted request at a fuel cap is excluded. Its inequality with answered cost is therefore in the correct direction and refers to the manuscript's attempted-call interpretation.

**Probability, decoding, and retained failures.** The perturbation law is the product of a uniform observer, a uniform real offset on `(-w,w]`, and Haar probability on the unit circle. Both center marginals are Haar, and their real representatives are atomless. `NonnegativeGeometry` proves the fixed-shift separation estimate by an explicit symmetric-difference cover. `NonnegativeShift` integrates `2|u|` to obtain the sharp bound `w`; the same law gives the equal-owner bound `1/(2nw)`. `NonnegativeDetection` obtains `w/n` per determining request and applies a union bound to the entire fixed baseline transcript. It never conditions an adaptive next request on previous nondetection.

For every actual uniform envy-free allocation, total share length and all pairwise comparisons imply equal ownership fibers of mass `1/n`. The independent observer contributes the exact exception factor `1-1/n`. The physical decoder makes the changed observer strictly envious unless it owns the positive center for cake or the negative center for chores. The real-valued decoder estimate correctly handles windows for which its displayed factor is negative; the ENNReal truncation is used in the favorable direction.

Every wrong complete uniform output has the positive measurable gap `max length - min length`. **Measurability and limits.** The tape is an arbitrary measurable probability space; there is no finite-support, countability, standard-Borel, or conditional-distribution requirement on it. Query, history, action, and allocation codes use their natural coordinate measurable structures. Variable finite output counts and all endpoint coordinates are measurable. Joint ownership, share lengths, physical pulse values, actual finite runs, and eventual success are proved measurable.

`NonnegativeMeasurableOracle` proves measurability of the actual unique Cut root using its explicit strict-sublevel characterization, rather than assuming a measurable selector. `NonnegativeSuccess.fixed_input_failure_bound` (line 265) then applies Tonelli to fixed-input guarantees with input parameters sampled independently of the tape.

The width argument needs only the finiteness of each endpoint list, with no uniform or expected component-count bound. `NonnegativeProbability.endpoint_loss_tendsto_zero` proves this from atomlessness and continuity of measure on decreasing events; the wrong-output gap loss tends to zero separately. A single deterministic width controls both centers and the gap. The formal proof uses widths `1/(m+1)`, which is an equally valid decreasing sequence in place of the paper's dyadic sequence.

The formal cap argument is also sound. Finite unconditional expectation implies finite query cost almost surely and hence a genuine terminal behavior at a finite cap. Exhaustion probability tends to zero even when the terminal behavior is divergence. `NonnegativeLower.all_windows` first chooses a cap with small exhaustion loss, then one width with small endpoint/gap loss for that cap, applies the fixed-input guarantee to every resulting concrete profile, and sends the total error to zero. The width may depend on the protocol and desired error, but never on a sampled input or tape. The single protocol is unchanged throughout. Infinite expectation is handled separately and immediately. This ordering avoids a measurable unbounded last-query-time selection and does not weaken the final claim.

**Remaining encoding correspondence.** `IntervalAllocation.Allocation` (line 11) carries boundedness, pairwise disjointness, and coverage as proof fields. Its records are half-open `(left,right]` intervals plus an explicit owner of point 0. The count is arbitrary and may be unbounded over tapes, and every real endpoint is free. However, an invalid or incomplete raw return is not a constructor of this output type. The manuscript explicitly counts such returns as failures and allows general finite-union interval encodings.

Mathematically, invalid returns can be replaced by query-free divergence after a valuation-independent geometric check, and the ownership of finitely many endpoints can be normalized without changing any nonatomic value. A literal machine-checked correspondence should prove that the chosen normalization is measurable, preserves successful physical values, and preserves or decreases unconditional query cost, then apply the public theorem to the normalized protocol. The new shared `OutputPostprocess` and `RawPartition` modules appear designed for this purpose, but `NonnegativeMain` does not import or instantiate them. `MixedRawMain` is a separate theorem and does not by itself close this nonnegative endpoint. A sorted-cut raw adapter would close malformed returns in that syntax; a claim covering other raw encodings should also state or prove their translation to it.

This qualification should not be inflated into a demand for a machine-language compiler or a proof that divergence is decidable. The manuscript itself specifies a semantic measurable next-action rule with query-free nonreturn. Likewise, serialized unknown query tags are outside the typed Eval/Cut syntax and should be described as an interface convention. The substantive outstanding correspondence item is the treatment of arbitrary raw finite output encodings at the public nonnegative endpoint.
