The new `UpperGlobalProtocolCostMeasurable` proves the actual supremum counts measurable. These are unconditional costs on the original stream, not costs conditioned on successful runs.

The exact policy contract is an answered-query-history policy, not access to private past random coordinates absent from that history. It covers the requested full-history ordinary Option oracle and the ordinary oracle interpretation of the manuscript. If “past” in a different interpretation grants an adversary additional publicly revealed internal random values, that stronger observation model needs its own adapter/statement. An explicit pushforward from one sequence of uniform real samples to every discrete/continuous tape coordinate is not part of the public endpoint inspected here. Such finite discrete samplers are elementary ideal-real operations; this is an encoding refinement, distinct from assuming independence of accepted attempts.
